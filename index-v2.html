§ 02 — About

Old knowledge,
kept simple.

Reidflora started with a simple question: why is it so hard to find functional mushrooms you can actually trust? We went looking for growers who cared as much as we did.

Today we keep a short list on purpose — only what we'd use ourselves, sourced with attention to purity, standards and consistency.

“Fewer things, done properly. That's the whole idea.”
